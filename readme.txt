Longer change history, CSV export and email alerts for important changes, for Sitekeel.

== Description ==

Sitekeel Pro extends the free Sitekeel plugin, which must be installed and active.

* **Longer history.** Keep changes for 6 months, 1 year, 2 years or for good, instead of the free plugin's 90 days. The timeline's period filter grows with it.
* **CSV export.** Download exactly the changes you are looking at - filtered by source, period or "important only" - for an audit, a client report or a spreadsheet.
* **Email alerts.** Get an email when something important happens: a plugin is deactivated, the theme switches, a Heralda bar is turned off, a ProofBlocks banner disappears from a live page, a product goes offline or out of stock, a price changes, an AI agent changes something. Choose which changes you care about. Alerts are grouped: at most one email every ten minutes.

== External services ==

Sitekeel Pro does not connect to any external service for its features. Alerts are sent with your site's own email setup (wp_mail). Licensing and updates are handled by Freemius once the plugin is activated with a license.

== Installation ==

1. Install and activate the free **Sitekeel** plugin.
2. Upload and activate **Sitekeel Pro**.
3. Open **Sitekeel → History & alerts** to choose how long to keep changes and which alerts to get.
